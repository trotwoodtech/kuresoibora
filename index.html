Dominic Kiprotich

Chairman and Founder, Kuresoi Bora Initiative

Dominic is a nutrition scientist, university lecturer and farmer. He teaches in the Department of Food, Nutrition and Dietetics at Kabarak University, and has spent years researching and running programmes that improve health in communities.

On his own farm he has tried new ways of growing potatoes, the main cash crop in Kuresoi South, so he knows the work, the cost and the risk that farming families carry. He founded Kuresoi Bora Initiative to turn good ideas into practical, shared action, and is seeking to serve as Member of Parliament for Kuresoi South.

  • MSc Nutrition, Kabarak University
  • BSc Human Nutrition and Dietetics, Egerton University
  • Lecturer, Kabarak University
  • Farmer

A word from Dominic

Kuresoi South is a place of hardworking people and rich land. Yet too many families still struggle to earn fairly from their farms, to keep their children in school, to reach a clinic that is close and stocked, or to travel on a road that stays open in the rains.

I started Kuresoi Bora Initiative because these problems will not wait. I will listen first, bring the right partners to the table, work openly, and report back to you on what has been done.

As a farmer, I know the effort that goes into every harvest. As a nutrition lecturer, I know how much a well-fed child and a healthy family can achieve. Let us build a better Kuresoi together.

Dominic KiprotichChairman and Founder, Kuresoi Bora Initiative
