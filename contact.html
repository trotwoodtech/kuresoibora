Common questions

What does the Initiative do?

We work with residents, government and other organisations to improve farming, education, health, infrastructure, governance and opportunities for youth and women in Kuresoi South.

How is my feedback used?

Feedback helps us decide where to focus, what to raise with government and partners, and how to improve. We aim to summarise what we hear and share what we do about it.

How can I volunteer?

Choose Volunteer in the form and tell us your ward and skills. We will contact you when activities are being organised.

Do you only work in Kuresoi South?

Our focus is Kuresoi South. Many challenges are shared with Kuresoi North, and we aim to work there too as we grow.
